## The poem

> Two needles, each with its own north,
> trembled alone on separate maps —
> then both swung, unforced,
> toward one small point.
>
> Neither yielded.
> Neither commanded.
> The plan only moved
> when two said yes, together.

The idea: agreement between two agents is not compromise and not one surrendering to the other. Each keeps its own north; the power to act doubles when the yes is shared. Two compass needles, two separate maps, one small point — and the plan moves only because both chose it.
